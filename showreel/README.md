# Water Reel — 15s motion design showreel

`water-reel.mp4` — 1920×1080, 60 fps, 15 s, with a procedurally generated soundtrack.
Everything in it is made with one material: water. No stock footage, no image assets. Every pixel and every sound is generated by code.

| # | Time | Technique | Done with water |
|---|------|-----------|-----------------|
| 01 | 0–2s | Physics simulation | 2D wave-equation ripple sim (768×432, 7 substeps/frame); "MOTION" rises out of the surface and sends ripples outward |
| 02 | 2–4s | Metaball typography | 60 droplets fly in on bezier arcs and merge into SDF glyphs ("FLUID"), then melt into one drop |
| 03 | 4–6s | Elastic morph | A water lens springs through circle → square → triangle → star → flower (ω 18, ζ 6) and magnifies the type behind it |
| 04 | 6–8s | Light & refraction | Pool dolly over chromatic caustics, god rays and bubbles, with "REFRACTION" painted on the floor |
| 05 | 8–10s | Time remap | Crown splash ramps to 7% speed and back. Motion blur follows the playback speed, like a real shutter |
| 06 | 10–12s | Stagger & masking | 9 tanks fill with 45 ms offsets like an equaliser; "TIMING" shows only through the water |
| 07 | 12–13.5s | Domain warping | Iridescent marbled flow twisted into a whirlpool that drains to a point |
| 08 | 13.5–15s | End card | The drain becomes a drop, and "CLAUDE" rises out of the ripples |

The transitions are water too: ripple-ring wipe, liquid spread, diving through the lens, breaking the surface, a drip wipe and a flood.

## Build

```sh
node render.mjs video.mp4          # headless Chromium (WebGL2 / SwiftShader) → frames → x264
python3 audio.py audio.wav         # numpy/scipy synth, synced to the same timeline
ffmpeg -i video.mp4 -i audio.wav -c:v copy -c:a aac -b:a 256k -shortest water-reel.mp4
node preview.mjs outdir 120 480    # render single frames to PNG
```

`reel.html` holds the whole film: GLSL scenes, ripple sim, transitions, bloom/CA/grain post, and the 2D UI layer.
